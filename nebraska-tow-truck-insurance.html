Tow Truck Insurance in Nebraska

Nebraska has no tow-specific rule — the general $25,000/$50,000/$25,000 motor-vehicle liability floor applies. The PSC’s $750,000 liability and $5,000 cargo figures cover household-goods carriers only, not tow operators.

Nebraska state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident bodily injury / $25,000 property damage — general motor-vehicle liability (proof of financial responsibility, Neb. Rev. Stat. § 60-501(14)). PSC § 003.03 amounts ($750,000 liability + $5,000 cargo for household-goods carriers; passenger-carrier tiers) do not apply to tow operators. NE adopts 49 CFR Part 387 via § 75-363 for intrastate carriers over 10,000 lbs, but § 387.3(a) limits the $750,000 subpart to interstate/foreign commerce for nonhazardous property, so its intrastate application to tow is unsettled in the sources read.
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
No PSC insurance filing established for tow operators. § 75-307 filing duty runs to certificated carriers; the former catch-all for non-certificated intrastate property carriers (§ 75-307.01) was repealed in 2007 (LB 358). PSC filing forms exist (Form E/G liability, H/J cargo, K/L cancellation — 291 NAC Ch. 3 § 006.05) but no filing requirement for tow operators was established.

Weight / class variation: PSC rule varies only by household-goods vs. passenger seating capacity; no tow or GVWR variation.

Workers’ comp in Nebraska

Required?

Independent-contractor test: Common-law multi-factor test from case law — no statutory IC definition in the WC Act. The WC statute (§ 48-115) defines "employee" but never defines "independent contractor." Nebraska Supreme Court applies a 10-factor balancing test (Restatement (Second) of Agency § 220). DIFFERENT from wage-payment law, which uses an ABC test (Neb. Rev. Stat. § 48-1229). For unemployment insurance, Nebraska does NOT use the WC 10-factor test: under Neb. Rev. Stat. § 48-604(5) (Employment Security Law), services performed for wages are deemed employment unless the employer shows to the satisfaction of the Commissioner of Labor all three ABC prongs — (a) freedom from control or direction both under contract and in fact, (b) service outside the usual course of the business or outside all places of business, and (c) the worker customarily engaged in an independently established trade, occupation, profession, or business — and the statute states this is not a codification of the common law.

Police rotation in Nebraska

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
