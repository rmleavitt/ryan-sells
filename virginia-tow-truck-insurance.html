Tow Truck Insurance in Virginia

Virginia tiers intrastate liability by weight: $750,000 above 10,000 lbs GVWR, $300,000 from 7,500 to 10,000 lbs, plus $50,000 cargo. Tow operators are motor carriers who must certify coverage to DMV before any plate or permit issues.

Virginia state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Intrastate (Va. Code § 46.2-2143.1(B)): GVWR over 10,000 lbs — $750,000; GVWR over 7,500 up to 10,000 lbs — $300,000; passenger cars, motorcycles, autocycles, and vehicles GVWR 7,500 lbs or less — § 46.2-472 financial responsibility ($50,000 per person / $100,000 per accident / $25,000 PD for policies effective on/after 1/1/2025) while available to transport, and $100,000 per person / $300,000 per incident BI/death + $50,000 PD while en route to pickup through delivery. Interstate: federal minimums (§ 46.2-2143.1(B)). Household-goods carriers: $750,000 (§ 46.2-2143.1(C)).
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Cargo
$50,000 cargo insurance required for intrastate motor carriers (§ 46.2-2143.1(D)). Carriers not engaged in household-goods transportation, and those solely operating passenger cars, motorcycles, autocycles, and vehicles GVWR 7,500 lbs or less, are not required to file cargo insurance proof (§ 46.2-2143.1(D)); DMV requires cargo proof only from property carriers transporting household goods.
Filings
No certificate of fitness, permit, registration card, or license plate issued until the motor carrier certifies coverage to DMV (§ 46.2-2143) — insurance policy/bond, certificate of insurance in lieu, letter of credit, or lessor statement, in the § 46.2-2143.1 amounts. Owner must declare for-hire operation to DMV before any vehicle is used to transport property for compensation (§ 46.2-2121.1). DMV's Motor Carrier Insurance Filing System accepts Form E (BI/PD), Form H (cargo), Form K (cancellation), Form G, Form J, and binders from insurers. For-hire intrastate (Property) permit applicants must file proof of minimum liability limits at application.

Weight / class variation: GVWR bands: 10,000+ lbs vs over 7,500–10,000 lbs vs 7,500 lbs or less (passenger cars, motorcycles, autocycles). The (i)/(ii) available-to-transport vs. en-route-through-delivery split applies to § 46.2-2101(6)-exempt carriers. No intrastate hazmat tier in § 46.2-2143.1; interstate = federal.

Tow operators are 'motor carriers' under § 46.2-2100 (property for compensation); the § 46.2-2101 exemptions do not exempt carriers from § 46.2-2143.1. The former BTRO regulation (24 VAC 27-30) is historical — the Board was abolished and its regulations repealed effective 1/1/2013 (2012 Acts chs. 803/835) — so its $50,000 on-hook / $50,000 garagekeepers figures are NOT current law and are not used. DCJS registers tow truck drivers only. Localities may regulate towing fees (§ 46.2-1233) but have no insurance-setting authority under that section. No intrastate hazmat tier in § 46.2-2143.1; interstate = federal. Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence).

Police rotation in Virginia

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
