01 / COVERAGE

The protection your operation depends on.

A towing policy is more than commercial auto. The right package considers your trucks, the vehicles you transport, your lot, your shop and the ways each part of the business creates risk.

Commercial auto

Liability and physical damage options for wreckers, rollbacks, service vehicles and support units.

On-hook towing

Protection for customers’ vehicles while they are attached to, transported by or being handled by your tow truck.

Garagekeepers

Coverage for vehicles in your care at the yard, storage facility or shop—where on-hook coverage may no longer apply.

Business protection

General liability, workers’ compensation, property and umbrella options to protect the operation beyond the road.

05 / QUESTIONS

Before you start.

A few details help make the first conversation more productive. If you do not have every document ready, start with what you know.

What information will I need?

Typically: a vehicle schedule, driver list, loss runs, current policy details, coverage limits, operating radius and a description of the services you provide.

Do you work with new towing businesses?

Yes—new towing ventures are welcome. We know which markets write new tow operations, and we can get you covered.

Can you insure a fleet in more than one state?

Ryan is licensed in all 50 states and can discuss multi-state operations. Carrier availability, filings and coverage terms vary by risk and jurisdiction.

Can I get coverage for customer vehicles?

On-hook towing and garagekeepers coverage address different phases of customer-vehicle custody. The right limits and terms depend on the vehicles you handle, where they are stored and the services you perform.
