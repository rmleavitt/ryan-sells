Tow Truck Insurance in Texas

Texas regulates towing through TDLR permit types — Incident Management, Private Property, and Consent towing — each with its own insurance minimums. Texas is also the only state where workers’ comp is optional for most private employers.

Texas state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$500,000 per truck per incident for Incident Management Towing; $300,000 per truck per incident for Private Property Towing and Consent Towing (TDLR tow-specific)
Garagekeepers
On-hook (in-tow)
$50,000 cargo / cargo-on-hook for Incident Management and Private Property Towing; not required for Consent Towing
Cargo
$50,000 cargo (TDLR lists as 'Cargo or Cargo on Hook') for Incident Management and Private Property Towing; not required for Consent Towing
Filings
Certificate of insurance filed in TDLR TOOLS system by insurer, with 30-day cancellation notice to TDLR; TxDMV Form E also applies where TxDMV intrastate motor-carrier authority applies (TxDMV 'All Others' over 26,000 lbs: $500,000)

Weight / class variation: Varies by TDLR permit type (Incident Management vs Private Property vs Consent), not by GVWR; TxDMV intrastate tier applies only over 26,000 lbs

Tow-specific TDLR rule. No TDLR garagekeepers minimum found.

Workers’ comp in Texas

Required?
No — optional
Trigger
N/A (nonsubscriber framework)
State fund only?
No

Independent-contractor test: Statutory four-element IC definition, Tex. Labor Code § 406.121(2) (Subchapter F). § 406.122(a): a person working for a general contractor or motor carrier that is an employer is an employee unless operating as an IC or employed by an IC.

Not required doesn’t mean not liable: if you don’t carry workers’ comp — even where it isn’t required for you — an injured worker may be able to sue you in court instead of being limited to comp benefits, because the exclusive-remedy shield generally depends on having coverage.

Police rotation in Texas

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
