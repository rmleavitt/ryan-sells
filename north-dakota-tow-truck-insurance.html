Tow Truck Insurance in North Dakota

North Dakota has no tow-specific or intrastate motor-carrier liability minimum — the general $25,000/$50,000/$25,000 financial-responsibility floor applies. Intrastate nonhazardous tow operations are not subject to the federal $750,000, which is limited to interstate commerce.

North Dakota state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
No tow-specific or intrastate motor-carrier liability minimum. General auto financial-responsibility floor: $25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident bodily injury / $25,000 property damage (NDCC 39-16.1-11(2)(b)). Intrastate nonhazardous tow operations are NOT subject to the federal $750,000: 49 CFR 387.3(a) limits the nonhazardous tier to interstate/foreign commerce, ND's adoption (NDAC 38-04-01-02) adds no intrastate extension, and NDCC ch. 49-18 (motor-carrier regulation) is repealed. Interstate for-hire tow: $750,000 at 10,001+ lbs GVWR via Part 387 (federal layer). Hazmat edge: Part 387 hazmat tiers ($5,000,000 bulk / $1,000,000 oil-other) DO apply intrastate per 387.3(b). Oversize mobile-home/modular-unit towing: $100,000 / $300,000 / $50,000 (NDAC 38-06-02-05) — permit-specific, not a general tow minimum.
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
No tow-operator insurance filing in North Dakota; no Form E; no intrastate operating-authority filing for tow (NDCC ch. 49-18 repealed). Only filing-adjacent items: written verification of liability/property-damage coverage for oversize-permit movements exceeding 200,000 lbs GVW or when required by the Highway Patrol / DOT engineers (NDAC 38-06-02-05(2)); an insurance bond, NDDOT as additional insured, may be required for excessive size/weight movements (38-06-02-05(3)). General proof-of-insurance (insurance card) requirements apply to all vehicles.

NDCC 39-16.1 is titled 'Proof of Financial Responsibility for the Future'; its 39-16.1-11(2)(b) limits are the operative definition of the minimum motor vehicle liability policy (25/50/25). NDAC 38-04-01-01 states the adopted regulations are binding on all motor carriers operating within or through the state — but because 38-04-01-03 adds no scope amendment, Part 387's own 387.3(a) interstate limitation controls for the nonhazardous tier. NDCC 39-32-02(1)(c) exempts tow-truck drivers operating at law-enforcement request from intrastate hours-of-service rules — hours-of-service only, no insurance content. The August 2026 ND Insurance Department towing study (SB 2374 § 16) concerned towing/recovery coverage for vehicles with liability-only insurance and recommended no legislation — it creates no tow-operator insurance requirement. No statewide Highway Patrol rotation insurance rule found; city/county or local rotation terms may set their own. Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence).

Independent-contractor test: Common-law right-to-control, codified in a WSI rule. Presumption of employment: anyone performing services for remuneration is presumed an employee unless proven an IC under the common-law test.

Not required doesn’t mean not liable: if you don’t carry workers’ comp — even where it isn’t required for you — an injured worker may be able to sue you in court instead of being limited to comp benefits, because the exclusive-remedy shield generally depends on having coverage.
