Tow Truck Insurance in Mississippi

Mississippi’s general intrastate rule adopts the federal Part 387 floor — but companies on the CTAC Tow List face higher figures: liability of at least $1,000,000, plus $250,000 on-hook and $250,000 garagekeepers. The state sets no general statewide on-hook or garagekeepers minimum.

Mississippi state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Intrastate for-hire: insurance in an amount required by the Transportation Commission, in no case less than USDOT 49 CFR Part 387 minimums (Miss. Code §§ 77-7-83, 77-7-85). General financial-responsibility floor 25/50/25 (§ 63-15-3(j)). CTAC tow-list (nonconsensual commercial-vehicle towing): liability ≥ $1,000,000 (CTAC Manual Rule 9(B) — official DPS copy, Ver. 1.5, published 4.6.26).
On-hook (in-tow)
$250,000 minimum for companies on the CTAC Tow List (nonconsensual towing of commercial motor vehicles) — CTAC Manual Rule 9(C) (official DPS copy, Ver. 1.5). No general statewide on-hook minimum in Miss. Code Title 77, Ch. 7.
Garagekeepers
$250,000 minimum for CTAC Tow List companies — CTAC Manual Rule 9(D) (official DPS copy, Ver. 1.5). No general statewide garagekeepers minimum in Title 77, Ch. 7.
Filings
Certificate of insurance (policy or certificate) filed with the Transportation Commission (§§ 77-7-83, 77-7-85); 10 days' written cancellation notice to the commission (§ 77-7-89). CTAC tow-list participants file a COI with DPS CTED-CTAC listing CTAC as certificate holder (CTAC Manual Rule 9(A) — official DPS copy, Ver. 1.5). Mississippi does not name Form E.

CTAC Manual Rule 9 figures confirmed from the official DPS copy (Ver. 1.5, published 4.6.26). § 77-7-23 is rulemaking authorization only — no commission rule setting a dollar amount for restricted carriers exists in the codified Admin Code (Title 37, Part 1, Subpart 6301 contains only Over-Dimensional Permits); the Part 387 floor comes from §§ 77-7-83/85. No cargo insurance minimum for tow operators; MDOT's $5,000/$10,000 cargo figures apply to household-goods/passenger Certificate carriers only. No $50k/$100k/$300k weight tiers appear in any Mississippi source read. Discrepancy disclosed: the manual's appendix inspection checklist (Section III. INSURANCE) still asks whether on-hook and garagekeepers limits are at least $500,000, inconsistent with Rule 9's $250,000 minimums. Rule 9 is the operative rule and controls; the checklist appears not to have been updated when Rule 9(C), (D) were modified in Ver. 1.4 (01/21/26). A CTAC inspector may still ask about $500,000 at inspection. Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence).
