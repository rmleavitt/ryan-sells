Tow Truck Insurance in Connecticut

Connecticut licenses tow operators as DMV wreckers, not PUC carriers. The general floor is $25,000/$50,000/$25,000, and for-hire carriers must evidence federal Part 387 amounts on the annual DMV filing. The state sets no on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum.

Connecticut state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability — General floor $25,000 per person / $50,000 per accident bodily injury / $25,000 property damage (CGS §§ 38a-335, 14-112(a)).
For-hire carriers must evidence 49 CFR Part 387 amounts on the annual DMV filing for § 14-163c(a) vehicles (intrastate 18,001+ lbs; interstate 10,001+ lbs): $750,000 for freight vehicles 10,001+ lbs GVWR, non-hazardous (CGS §§ 14-163c, 14-163d(e); CT DMV guidance).
On-hook (in-tow) — No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings — Annual DMV filing of evidence of security/insurance (CGS § 14-163d(a)) for § 14-163c(a) vehicles
Acord 25 Certificate of Liability Insurance per DMV guidance; for-hire carriers evidence Part 387 amounts (§ 14-163d(e)). No Form E. Repairer's license requires a $25,000 surety bond (§ 14-52(b)(1)) — a license bond, not insurance.

Weight / class variation: § 14-163c(a) vehicles: intrastate 18,001+ lbs; interstate 10,001+ lbs; hazmat; 8+ passenger for compensation. Part 387 amounts evidenced on the filing.

Tow operators are DMV-licensed wreckers under § 14-66 (dealer/repairer license required), not PUC-certificated carriers — the prior entry's § 16-304-D5 figures were the PUC-certificated carrier schedule (and the $100k/$300k/$50k tier was the interstate under-10,000 schedule), so they are not used here. Operative rule: §§ 14-163c/14-163d + Part 387 amounts. No state on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum in § 14-66, the wrecker regs (§§ 14-63-34–37b), or the State Police rotation regs (§§ 29-23a-1–17). Related but distinct: repairer's license requires a $25,000 surety bond (§ 14-52(b)(1)) — a license bond, not insurance. Municipal rotation agreements may set their own figures. Police rotation in Connecticut

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
