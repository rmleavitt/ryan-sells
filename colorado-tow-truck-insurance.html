Tow Truck Insurance in Colorado

Colorado has a tow-specific rule: $750,000 combined single limit for towing carriers at any GVWR. Cargo and garagekeepers coverage are mandatory, but the rule sets no dollar floor for them.

Colorado state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$750,000 combined single limit, towing carriers, any GVWR (4 CCR 723-6, Rule 6007(a)(I))
On-hook (in-tow)
Required as part of cargo liability - covers physical damage to motor vehicle in tow (on-hook) and loss of its contents; no dollar minimum stated, to extent of legal liability
Garagekeepers
Required for towing carriers providing storage directly or through an agent; no dollar minimum stated, to extent of legal liability
Cargo
Required; includes on-hook (physical damage to vehicle in tow + contents); no dollar minimum stated for towing carriers, to extent of legal liability
Filings
Form E or Form G (liability); Form H or Form J, or Colorado Form 12-INS (cargo); Colorado Form 14-INS (garagekeepers); filed with PUC

Weight / class variation: None for towing carriers - $750,000 applies at any GVWR

Tow-specific rule exists. Cargo/garagekeepers are mandatory but the rule sets no dollar floor for towing carriers (movers' $10,000 cargo floor does not apply to towers).

Police rotation in Colorado

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
