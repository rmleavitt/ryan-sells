Tow Truck Insurance in Washington

Washington has a tow-specific Registered Tow Truck Operator rule: $100,000 liability per occurrence plus $50,000 garagekeepers legal liability per occurrence for vehicle damage in custody. The state sets no on-hook or cargo minimum.

Washington state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$100,000 liability for bodily injury or property damage per occurrence (RCW 46.55.030(3)(a))
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Garagekeepers
$50,000 legal liability per occurrence for vehicle damage (fire/theft) from custody until redeemed/sold — DOL labels this garage keepers insurance (RCW 46.55.030(3)(b))
Filings
Proof of insurance to DOL before registration certificate issues (RCW 46.55.030(3)); no Form E name in statute

Workers’ comp in Washington

Yes — monopolistic

Independent-contractor test: Statutory six-part test, RCW 51.08.195 — all six must be met. It is an exception to the "worker" (RCW 51.08.180) and "employer" (RCW 51.08.070) definitions.

Not required doesn’t mean not liable: if you don’t carry workers’ comp — even where it isn’t required for you — an injured worker may be able to sue you in court instead of being limited to comp benefits, because the exclusive-remedy shield generally depends on having coverage.

Police rotation in Washington

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
