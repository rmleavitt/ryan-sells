Tow Truck Insurance in Wyoming

Wyoming treats tow/recovery vehicles as contract carriers: $750,000 CSL liability plus $10,000 cargo. Rotation-list carriers face class-tiered on-hook/cargo and garagekeepers of $75,000 to $200,000 — figures that do not apply to non-rotation operators.

Wyoming state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$750,000 CSL liability (Form E) for contract motor carriers (045-3 Wyo. Code R. § 3-3(a)). Tow/recovery vehicles are contract carriers.
On-hook (in-tow)
Rotation-list only: combined on-hook/cargo minimum — Class A and R: $75,000; Class B: $150,000; Class C: $200,000 (WYDOT Ch. 1, App. B § 9(c)(ii)). No statewide on-hook minimum for non-rotation operators.
Garagekeepers
Rotation-list only: Class A and R: $75,000; Class B: $150,000; Class C: $200,000 (WYDOT Ch. 1, App. B § 9(c)(iii)). No statewide garagekeepers minimum for non-rotation operators.
Cargo
$10,000 cargo (Form H) for common/contract carriers, except carriers of commodities with no appreciable salvage value (045-3 Wyo. Code R. § 3-3(b), § 3-2(b)). Rotation-list: combined on-hook/cargo — Class A/R $75,000; Class B $150,000; Class C $200,000 (App. B § 9(c)(ii)).
Filings
Form E (liability) and Form H (cargo) filed with WYDOT Regulatory Section; certificates of insurance not accepted for WYDOT filings (Ch. 3 § 10(d)); cancellation on 30 days' written notice (Form K) (Ch. 3 § 11). Rotation-list: itemized certificate of insurance filed with WHP before placement, showing vehicle liability, garage keeper's, on-hook, and cargo amounts (App. B § 9(b)).

Weight / class variation: Rotation-list on-hook/cargo and garagekeepers vary by wrecker class: A/R $75,000; B $150,000; C $200,000. General contract-carrier liability does not vary by class.

Tow/recovery vehicles are contract carriers (WYDOT Operating Authority Manual; Ch. 1 § 6(g)). General contract-carrier rule: Form E $750,000 CSL + Form H $10,000 cargo (045-3 Wyo. Code R. § 3-3, amended eff. 6/8/2016; the older WYDOT Chapter 3 PDF still showing $500,000 is a pre-amendment printing). The WHP rotational-list manual (Ch. 1, App. B § 9) additionally requires rotation-list tow carriers to carry vehicle liability $750,000 (all classes), combined on-hook/cargo $75,000 (A/R) / $150,000 (B) / $200,000 (C), and garagekeepers $75,000 (A/R) / $150,000 (B) / $200,000 (C), with an itemized certificate filed with WHP. No on-hook or garagekeepers minimum applies to non-rotation operators. Umbrella policy acceptable if it covers all four categories at ≥ the sum of the category minimums (App. B § 9(d)). Not required doesn’t mean not liable: if you don’t carry workers’ comp — even where it isn’t required for you — an injured worker may be able to sue you in court instead of being limited to comp benefits, because the exclusive-remedy shield generally depends on having coverage.

Police rotation in Wyoming

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
