Tow Truck Insurance in Tennessee

Tennessee’s general rule sets $750,000 at 10,000+ lbs GVWR and $300,000 under — but THP Towing List operators must carry $1,000,000 vehicle liability plus class-tiered on-hook and garagekeepers of $75,000 to $200,000 per incident. Those list figures are rotation requirements, not general statewide minimums.

Tennessee state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$750,000 for motor carriers/contract haulers with GVWR 10,000+ lbs; $300,000 for those under 10,000 lbs GVWR (property, nonhazardous) — Tenn. Comp. R. & Regs. 1340-06-01-.07(1)(a) & (e). THP Towing List operators must also carry $1,000,000 vehicle liability per incident (Towing Service Standards Manual § IX.B.3.a — flat across all classes). Hazmat tiers $1,000,000 / $5,000,000 (.07(1)(b)–(d)).
On-hook (in-tow)
Towing List only: $75,000 (Class A & D) / $150,000 (Class B) / $200,000 (Class C) per incident — THP Towing Service Standards Manual § IX.B.3.c (Revised January 2017, official tn.gov PDF). No statewide on-hook minimum for non-list tow operators in 1340-06-01.
Garagekeepers
Towing List only: $75,000 (Class A & D) / $150,000 (Class B) / $200,000 (Class C) per incident — THP Towing Service Standards Manual § IX.B.3.b. No statewide garagekeepers minimum for non-list operators in 1340-06-01.
Cargo
$5,000 per vehicle / $10,000 aggregate — Motor Common Carriers (intrastate only), 1340-06-01-.07(3). Applies to tow operators operating as common carriers (holding out to the public); tow operators working exclusively under individual contracts are 'contract haulers' (T.C.A. § 65-15-102) and fall outside the cargo rule.
Filings
Policy/certificate or surety bond filed with and approved by Commissioner of Safety; 30 days' written cancellation notice (1340-06-01-.06); FMCSA insurance/surety forms apply to all carriers including intrastate. THP Towing List: certificate of insurance filed with DOSHS before placement, itemized liability/garagekeepers/on-hook, policy valid at least 6 months, renewed before expiration (Manual § IX.B.1, § IX.C).

Weight / class variation: GVWR 10,000 lbs threshold; hazmat tiers $1,000,000 / $5,000,000. Towing List on-hook/garagekeepers vary by class: A & D $75,000; B $150,000; C $200,000.

THP Towing Service Standards Manual § IX figures now confirmed from the official tn.gov PDF (Revised January 2017 — the version currently published by DOSHS; no newer revision found): vehicle liability $1,000,000 flat all classes; on-hook and garagekeepers $75,000 (A & D) / $150,000 (B) / $200,000 (C), per incident. These are Towing List (rotation) requirements, not general statewide minimums. General rule: 1340-06-01 (auto $750k at 10,000+ / $300k under; cargo $5k/$10k for Motor Common Carriers intrastate only). Do not confuse with Knoxville PD municipal rotation rules (vehicle liability 300/500/750; city-only). Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence) from official TN sources.

Police rotation in Tennessee

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
