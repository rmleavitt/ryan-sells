Tow Truck Insurance in Michigan

Michigan’s $750,000 figure applies intrastate only to wreckers that are commercial motor vehicles at 26,001+ lbs — lighter intrastate wreckers fall under Michigan no-fault instead. The state sets no tow-specific on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum.

Michigan state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Two-tier. (1) Wreckers that are commercial motor vehicles under 49 CFR 383.5 (GVWR/GCW 26,001+ lbs, 16+ passengers, or hazmat placard): Michigan adopts 49 CFR Part 387 (MCL 480.11a) — $750,000 for for-hire nonhazardous property; hazmat $5,000,000 / $1,000,000. (2) Lighter wreckers in intrastate commerce (not 383.5 CMVs): Part 387 does NOT apply (MCL 480.11a(3)); Michigan no-fault applies — residual liability (MCL 500.3009: $250,000 / $500,000 BI default, electable down to $50,000 / $100,000; $10,000 PD) plus property protection insurance (PPI, benefits capped at $1,000,000 per accident, MCL 500.3121(6)) and PIP (MCL 500.3101). No $300,000 tier exists in 49 CFR 387.9.
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
Proof of active liability insurance submitted to MSP CVED for intrastate operating authority (towing companies must submit active liability insurance, per Michigan Attorney General towing page). Form E not confirmed from an official MSP source — not asserted here.

Weight / class variation: Part 387 applies intrastate only to 383.5 CMVs (26,001+ lbs GVWR/GCW, 16+ passengers, hazmat placard). No-fault limits apply to all other registered motor vehicles. Caution: 383.5 uses GVWR/GCW; whether a wrecker-plus-towed-vehicle combination counts toward the 26,001-lb GCW threshold was not settled in the sources read.

Michigan adopts 49 CFR Part 387 (MCL 480.11a), but 480.11a(3) exempts non-CMV vehicles in intrastate commerce — so the $750,000 figure applies intrastate only to 383.5 CMVs. The $300,000 under-10,001-lb figure sometimes stated for Michigan does not exist in 49 CFR 387.9 and is removed. The circulated weight-tier figures ($300k at 40,000+ lbs, $100k at 35,000–39,999, $50k under 35,000) do not appear in Michigan law. No Michigan tow-specific on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum. MSP rotation-list (No Preference Geographic Towing List) insurance specifics were not confirmed from an official source. HB 5146 (2025, pending) addresses towing rates/practices, not insurance minimums. Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence); filings stated as proof-of-insurance requirement without a named form.

Police rotation in Michigan

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
