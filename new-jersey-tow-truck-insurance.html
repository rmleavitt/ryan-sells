Tow Truck Insurance in New Jersey

New Jersey has tow-specific limits — $750,000 for light-medium-duty tow trucks and $1,000,000 for heavy-duty — but its general commercial-vehicle law reaches higher: combined, the operative rule is $750,000 up to 26,001 lbs GVWR and $1,500,000 above. The state sets no on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum.

New Jersey state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Tow-specific (N.J.S.A. 39:3-84.8): $750,000 single limit per accident/occurrence for each light-medium duty tow truck (<32,000 lbs GVW); $1,000,000 for each heavy-duty tow truck (≥32,000 lbs GVW). General commercial-motor-vehicle law (N.J.S.A. 39:6B-1, P.L. 2023, c. 276, eff. July 1, 2024): $300,000 for CMVs 10,001–<26,001 lbs GVWR; $1,500,000 for CMVs 26,001+ lbs GVWR. Combined operative rule for NJ-registered/principally garaged tow trucks (the higher of the two controls): $750,000 up to 26,001 lbs GVWR; $1,500,000 above 26,001 lbs GVWR.
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
Tow-truck registration application must include a valid certificate of insurance and schedule of insured vehicles showing the amounts of garage keeper's legal liability coverage and any on-hook coverage (N.J.S.A. 39:3-84.8). No Form E. N.J.S.A. 39:6B-1 is a maintain-coverage requirement with no filing form.

Weight / class variation: Tow-specific splits at 32,000 lbs GVW (light-medium vs. heavy-duty). General law splits at 26,001 lbs GVWR. Combined rule: $750,000 up to 26,001 lbs GVWR; $1,500,000 above.

Tow-specific limits (39:3-84.8) verified from P.L. 2007, c. 193. NJ's general CMV law (P.L. 2023, c. 276, N.J.S.A. 39:6B-1, eff. July 1, 2024) applies to tow trucks — no tow exemption; CMV definition includes commercially registered vehicles — and its $1,500,000 tier (26,001+ lbs GVWR) exceeds the tow-specific $1,000,000 heavy-duty figure. Combined rule (higher controls, per DOBI banding): $750,000 up to 26,001 lbs GVWR; $1,500,000 above. Note the split bases differ: tow-specific splits at 32,000 lbs GVW; general law splits at 26,001 lbs GVWR. 39:6B-1 reaches vehicles registered or principally garaged in NJ, including NJ-garaged vehicles traveling interstate (DOBI). No statewide on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum — 39:3-84.8 requires only disclosure of those coverage amounts; municipal rotation rules set their own.
