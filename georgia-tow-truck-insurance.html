Tow Truck Insurance in Georgia

Georgia’s nonconsensual towing permit rule sets $100,000/$300,000/$50,000 liability plus $50,000 garagekeepers/storage liability, with certificates filed annually with DPS.

Georgia state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability — $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident bodily injury / $50,000 property damage (intrastate, nonconsensual towing permit, DPS Rule 570-36-.09)
On-hook (in-tow) — No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Garagekeepers — $50,000 minimum garage keepers/storage liability, plus sufficient to cover actual value of all stored vehicles and contents
Filings — Certificates of Insurance furnished to DPS annually
30-day advance cancellation notice; no lapse allowed

Weight / class variation: Interstate: $1,000,000 liability per 49 CFR 387.7(a) + MCS-90 (DPS wrecker qualifications)

How we verified these numbers ▸

Tow-specific rule exists for nonconsensual towing permits. No state on-hook or cargo minimum stated in Rule 570-36-.09.

Police rotation in Georgia

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
