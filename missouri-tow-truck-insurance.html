Tow Truck Insurance in Missouri

Missouri has a tow-specific $500,000 per-incident requirement for towing companies, verified in the statute text. The statute sets no on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum and no GVWR variation.

Missouri state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$500,000 per incident/occurrence for death/injury and property damage (Mo. Rev. Stat. § 304.154(1)(7), towing companies under §§ 304.153/304.155/304.157)
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
Tow statute requires maintaining a valid policy/bond (no form named); MoDOT intrastate for-hire filings are Form E or G (bodily injury/property damage) and Form H or J (cargo)

Police rotation in Missouri

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
