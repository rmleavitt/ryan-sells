Tow Truck Insurance in Kentucky

Kentucky’s general motor-carrier property rule applies to tow trucks: $100,000/$300,000/$50,000 at 18,000 lbs GVW or less, $100,000/$600,000/$50,000 above. No tow-specific insurance amounts were found in the towing statutes.

Kentucky state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$100,000 per person / $300,000 total per accident / $50,000 property damage for property vehicles 18,000 lbs GVW or less; $100,000 / $600,000 / $50,000 for over 18,000 lbs (KRS 281.655(4))
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
Bond/policy or certificate of insurance filed with the Transportation Cabinet, with state insurance endorsement, per KRS 281.655(1)

Weight / class variation: GVW split at 18,000 lbs in 281.655(4); $1,000,000 CSL for hazmat vehicles in 281.655(6)

No tow-specific insurance amounts found in KRS 281.920-281.936 towing provisions; general motor-carrier property rule applied. Police rotation in Kentucky

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
