Tow Truck Insurance in New Hampshire

New Hampshire does not require auto insurance to operate — it runs a financial-responsibility system, and any policy issued must be at least $25,000/$50,000/$25,000. The only tow insurance rule in state law is the State Police tow-list rule: $1,000,000 liability including garagekeepers.

New Hampshire state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
No statewide operating minimum — NH does not require auto insurance to operate (financial-responsibility system, RSA 264); a motor vehicle liability policy, if issued, must be at least $25,000/$50,000/$25,000 (RSA 259:61). State Police tow-list operators: $1,000,000 liability incl. garagekeepers (RSA 106-B:30, XVI).
On-hook (in-tow)
Required for State Police tow-list participants, but no dollar minimum designated in NH law or admin rule (RSA 106-B:30, XVI).
Garagekeepers
$1,000,000 liability including garagekeepers coverage — State Police tow-list participants (RSA 106-B:30, XVI).
Filings
No named insurance filing form; tow-list application to the director of State Police (RSA 106-B:30, I); FMCSA safety registration + active USDOT + NH registration required (RSA 106-B:30, XXI).

NH has no general intrastate for-hire property-carrier insurance statute — RSA 375 (1967), RSA 375-B (2013), and RSA 375-A (2023) are all repealed, and the implementing Saf-C 4000 rules expired in 2006. NH does not require auto insurance to operate; RSA 264 is after-the-fact financial responsibility. The only tow insurance rule in state law is the State Police tow-list rule (RSA 106-B:30, XVI). Cargo: no minimum in RSA 106-B:30 (read in full) or the RSA 262 provisions reviewed. Municipal rotation lists may set their own requirements. Independent-contractor test: Statutory 7-criteria test — RSA 281-A:2, VI(b)(1). DIFFERENT from UI, which uses a 3-prong ABC test (RSA 282-A:9, III).

Not required doesn’t mean not liable: if you don’t carry workers’ comp — even where it isn’t required for you — an injured worker may be able to sue you in court instead of being limited to comp benefits, because the exclusive-remedy shield generally depends on having coverage.

Police rotation in New Hampshire

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
