Tow Truck Insurance in Arkansas

Arkansas has a tow-specific rule from its Towing & Recovery Board: tiered on-hook/cargo coverage of $50,000 light-duty, $100,000 medium-duty, and $200,000 heavy-duty, plus $50,000 garagekeepers. Auto liability follows the federal Part 387 schedule.

Arkansas state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Per state and federal minimum financial responsibility laws for motor carriers, incorporating 49 CFR 387.9 (27 CAR § 170-109(b)(1)). The rule text cites '45 C.F.R. § 387.9' — an evident scrivener's error, since Part 387 lives in Title 49.
On-hook (in-tow)
$50,000 Light Duty / $100,000 Medium Duty / $200,000 Heavy Duty cargo, on-hook, or similar insurance (27 CAR § 170-109(b)(2)(A); GVWR bands per § 170-107(d)(1)(A): Light 9,000–15,000 lbs; Medium 15,001–33,000 lbs; Heavy 33,001+ lbs)
Garagekeepers
$50,000 liability for personal injury/property damage in the area where impounded vehicles are stored (e.g., Garagekeepers Legal Liability) (27 CAR § 170-109(b)(3))
Cargo
$50,000 Light Duty / $100,000 Medium Duty / $200,000 Heavy Duty — single combined cargo, on-hook, or similar insurance requirement (27 CAR § 170-109(b)(2)(A))
Filings
Valid Certificate of Insurance on file with the Board reflecting § 170-109 coverage; COI issued to the Board, describing cancellation/material-change notice provisions, and displaying impound lot locations (§ 170-109(a), (c)). No Form E.

Weight / class variation: On-hook/cargo tiers by duty class (Light 9,000–15,000 lbs; Medium 15,001–33,000 lbs; Heavy 33,001+ lbs GVWR). Auto liability incorporates 49 CFR 387.9 GVWR/commodity tiers.

Tow-specific rule (Arkansas Towing & Recovery Board, 27 CAR Part 170). Tiered on-hook/cargo figures confirmed adopted — rules effective August 23, 2023, codified at 27 CAR § 170-109(b)(2)(A). Verified 2026-10-14: all fields confirmed from the current codified Arkansas rule.

Police rotation in Arkansas

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
