Tow Truck Insurance in Florida

Florida sets tow-truck minimums by vehicle weight under F.S. 627.7415, and FHP rotation adds its own layer — including a $50,000 tier that applies to all wreckers under 35,000 lbs. Workers’ comp kicks in at 4+ employees for non-construction businesses like towing.

Florida state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Commercial motor vehicles (F.S. 627.7415, additional coverage): $50,000 per occurrence at 26,000–34,999 lbs GVW; $100,000 at 35,000–43,999 lbs; $300,000 at 44,000+ lbs. CMVs subject to 49 CFR Part 387 must carry the federal minimums. FHP rotation: the wrecker rule applies the $50,000 tier to ALL wreckers under 35,000 lbs GVW (including those under 26,000 lbs), $100,000 at 35,000–43,999, $300,000 at 44,000+ (Rule 15B-9.0031(3)(d)1.d).
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Garagekeepers
Rotation-list only (FHP): garage keeper's legal liability insurance, $50,000 for each loss, covering fire and explosion; theft of a vehicle, its parts or contents; riot and civil commotion; vandalism; malicious mischief; and damage to a vehicle in tow (Rule 15B-9.0031(3)(d)1.c). No statewide garagekeepers minimum for non-rotation operators.
Filings
No Form E for tow operators. FHP rotation: certificates of insurance + 'Wrecker Insurance Statement of Compliance' (HSMV form 60313) furnished to the troop commander before approval for the rotation list and 30 days before policy expiration; no material change or cancellation effective without 30 days' prior written notice to the Division (Rule 15B-9.0031(3)(d)4).

Weight / class variation: 627.7415 tiers split at 26,000 / 35,000 / 44,000 lbs GVW (applies at 26,000+ lbs only; no tier below 26,000). Rotation rule applies $50,000 to all wreckers under 35,000 lbs.

F.S. 627.7415 is a general commercial-motor-vehicle rule (additional coverage), not tow-specific; it sets no tier below 26,000 lbs GVW. F.S. 323.002's $300,000 liability / $50,000 on-hook cargo figures are disclosure items for unauthorized wreckers in county/municipal systems (disclose whether the coverage is in effect), not operating minimums — and 323.002 contains no garagekeepers figure. FHP rotation (Rule 15B-9.0031(3)(d)1, eff. 1/6/2026; formerly cited as 15B-9.006) additionally requires authorized wreckers to carry garage liability $300,000 CSL, garage keeper's legal liability $50,000 per loss, and workers' comp per Florida law, and applies the $50,000 liability tier to all wreckers under 35,000 lbs. General FL financial-responsibility proof is 10/20/10 (F.S. 324.021) plus PIP/PD for registration — not tow-specific. Verified 2026-10-14: all fields resolved (figure or confirmed absence).

Police rotation in Florida

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
