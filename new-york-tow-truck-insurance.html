Tow Truck Insurance in New York

New York’s statewide floor is a $300,000 combined single limit for tow trucks — and New York City adds its own DCWP licensing layer on top.

New York state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
Statewide floor (VTL § 311(4)(a), § 345(b)(3)): tow-truck owner's/motor-vehicle liability policy must provide a combined single limit of at least $300,000 per accident for bodily injury/death/property damage. NYC (DCWP tow company license, Admin. Code § 20-500): $200,000 injury/death per person / $500,000 per accident / $50,000 property damage, OR $1,000,000 combined single limit.
On-hook (in-tow)
$25,000 for damage to a vehicle in the care, custody and control of the insured (VTL § 311(4)(a), § 345(b)(3) — statewide). NYC DCWP: same $25,000, stated as 'on hook' coverage under the $1,000,000 CSL option and as care/custody/control under the split-limits option.
Filings
No Form E for tow operators. Statewide: proof of financial security under VTL Article 6 (owner's policy, certificate of insurance, financial security bond, deposit, or self-insurer qualification — § 311(3), (5)). NYC DCWP: proof of insurance at licensing — certificate of liability insurance from the insurer within 30 days, or policy declarations page within 60 days, or broker certificate within 30 days + notarized Certification by Insurance Broker or Agent — plus notice to DCWP within 10 days if the policy is cancelled, expires, or is terminated.

Weight / class variation: Statewide VTL floor applies to all tow trucks; NYC DCWP adds its own licensing layer on top. The NYC split-limits option (200/500/50) vs. the state $300,000 CSL floor is a harmonization question — both layers reported as written.

Statewide tow-truck minimum exists in VTL § 311(4)(a) and § 345(b)(3): $300,000 CSL + $25,000 care/custody/control. NYC DCWP (Admin. Code § 20-500) adds its own licensing layer: 200/500/50 + $25,000 CCC, or $1,000,000 CSL + $25,000 on-hook. No state or NYC garagekeepers or cargo minimum; local ordinances set their own (e.g., Brookhaven, North Hempstead, Lloyd).
