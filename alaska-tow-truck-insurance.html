Tow Truck Insurance in Alaska

Alaska requires $500,000 bodily injury/death plus $200,000 property damage per occurrence for commercial freight carriers, with proof filed with DOT&PF. The state sets no on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum.

Alaska state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$500,000 bodily injury/death + $200,000 property damage per single occurrence (AS 19.10.300(a)). 17 AAC 25.210(b)(1) routes intrastate-only carriers to AS 19.10.300 levels — except an intrastate carrier described in 49 CFR 387.9 schedule item (2) (bulk hazmat: $5,000,000), which must meet that schedule. Interstate for-hire carriers remain subject to the federal 49 CFR 387.9 $750,000 (tool's federal layer).
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Filings
Evidence of security filed with DOT&PF (Division of Measurement Standards and Commercial Vehicle Compliance): policy/certificate of insurance from an insurer acceptable to the department, surety bond from a state-licensed surety, evidence of self-insurance accepted by the department, or other department-approved security. No policy/bond/security may be canceled on less than 30 days' written notice to the department, measured from the department's receipt. Driver must carry proof of insurance in the vehicle and display it to the other party after an accident. Intrastate-only motor carriers must also file a motor carrier identification report (MCS-150) with FMCSA, obtain an intrastate USDOT number, and update it every 24 months. No Form E.

Weight / class variation: AS 19.10.300(f)(1) defines 'commercial motor vehicle' for this section only as GVWR/GCWR greater than 26,000 lbs (or >15 passengers). But the operative subsection (a) also has a second, unqualified clause — 'a person who carries freight in a motor vehicle for commercial purposes' — with no weight limit on its face. The $500k/$200k figure is presented as the for-hire/commercial freight rule, with the §(f) definition noted, not as a rule that applies only above 26,000 lbs. General floor for vehicles outside this section remains AS 28.22.101 (50/100/25).

Citation corrected: the prior entry's 1985 SLA Ch. 104 / AS 28.33.010 is dead law — AS 28.33.010 was repealed by § 16, E.O. 98 (1997); the current codification is AS 19.10.300. Figures unchanged and confirmed as current law in the 2025 Alaska Statutes. No state on-hook, garagekeepers, or cargo minimum: the term 'on-hook' appears zero times in 17 AAC 25 and in none of the governing statutes; 'garage' appears in 17 AAC 25 only in out-of-service/repair contexts; the adopted 49 CFR Part 387 cargo provisions (Subpart C) apply only to household-goods carriers. AS 19.10.300(f)(2) defines 'freight' as 'commodities, articles, and cargo' for the liability section only — a definition, not a cargo-insurance mandate. Anchorage municipal bid docs (IFB-5184-26) requiring $1M garagekeeper's liability are a municipal procurement term, not a statewide minimum.
