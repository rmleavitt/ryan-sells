Tow Truck Insurance in Nevada

Nevada regulates tow cars through the Transportation Authority: $750,000 per person / per accident / property damage under the ’Freight only’ row, plus $15,000 per vehicle / $30,000 aggregate cargo. The state sets no on-hook or garagekeepers minimum.

Nevada state minimums

Verified from state law
Auto liability
$750,000 per person / $750,000 per accident (all persons) / $750,000 property damage (excluding cargo) — 'Freight only' row of NAC 706.191(1)(a). Any of the three amounts may be a single combined limit. Tow car operators must secure the insurance required by NAC 706.191 (NAC 706.1376(2)(l)). General auto financial-responsibility floor is $25,000/$50,000/$20,000 (NRS 485.185). If federal minimums rise above these amounts, the carrier must meet the federal amount (NAC 706.191(3)).
On-hook (in-tow)
No statewide minimum — your city/county or rotation agreement may still set one
Cargo
$15,000 per vehicle / $30,000 aggregate — cargo insurance for loss of or damage to property of shippers/consignees coming into the carrier's possession (NAC 706.191(2)). Tow car operators must secure the insurance required by NAC 706.191 (NAC 706.1376(2)(l)).
Filings
Evidence of insurance filed with the Nevada Transportation Authority on Form E (BI/PD) and Cargo Form H when applicable, before certificate/permit issuance (NAC 706.193(1)(a)); carrier must keep evidence current for each vehicle (NAC 706.191(4)); changes reported within 30 days (NAC 706.193(3)).

Tow cars are NTA-regulated (certificate of public convenience and necessity); NAC 706.1376(2)(l) requires tow-car applicants to secure the insurance required by NAC 706.191. The 706.191 'Freight only' row sets $750,000/$750,000/$750,000 (any may be a single combined limit) plus $15,000/$30,000 cargo. Do not confuse with NAC 706.288's GVWR-tiered $300k/$750k schedule — that governs DMV-side carriers (other than fully regulated), not tow cars. No on-hook or garagekeepers minimum anywhere in NAC Chapter 706 (confirmed absence). The $50k/$100k/$300k weight tiers, the $350,000 NTA CSL, the $50,000 garagekeepers claim, and the $1.5M SB 180 figure (2025 bill died in Assembly committee) do not appear in Nevada law and are not used. Police rotation in Nevada

Rotation is a local contract, not state law. Rotation agreements commonly require auto liability around $1,000,000 CSL, on-hook/cargo $50,000–$100,000, garagekeepers $50,000–$500,000, and the agency named as additional insured — check your agency’s agreement for its exact terms.
